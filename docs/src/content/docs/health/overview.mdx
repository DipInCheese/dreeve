Dreeve is built around the data your Garmin watch collects. Once [Garmin is connected](/integrations/garmin-connect/),
it fills itself: sleep, heart rate, HRV, stress, Body Battery, steps and weigh-ins arrive every hour, next to your
activities.

## Your normal

A resting heart rate of 52 says little on its own. Whether it is good news depends on what is normal **for you**.
So every number in Dreeve is shown next to your own normal:

- **Your normal** is the middle of your values over the 60 days before, give or take a margin that a single odd
  night can not move. It needs at least 14 days of history; until then a card says "Not enough history yet".
- **Within / above / below your normal** tells where today's value falls. Green means better than usual, amber
  worse than usual, grey neither, depending on the metric (a higher HRV is good, a higher resting heart rate is not).
- For HRV, Garmin's own balanced range is used: it is based on weeks of your overnight readings.

## The pages

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Last night and today at a glance: sleep, Body Battery (where you are now and how far you charged), HRV, resting heart rate, stress, steps and training readiness, plus your latest activity and weigh-in. |
| **Health** | One page per topic (sleep, heart, stress & Body Battery, body, fitness) with charts over 7 days, 30 days, 3 months or a year. The shaded band is your normal. |
| **Insights** | Your last 7 days: the average of every metric next to your normal and the week before, your sleep rhythm and a training summary. |
| **Training** | Activities, calendar, training load, best efforts, heatmap, gear and more. |

Everything is in metric units.

## How fresh it is

Garmin data is synced [every hour](/integrations/garmin-connect/#how-syncing-works), and imported within seconds of
arriving. The top bar shows when the last sync was. Your watch first has to sync with Garmin Connect, so data from
the last hour may not be there yet; the sync button on the Garmin page fetches it right away.

## Where it is stored

Everything stays on your own server, in your own folder: `data/<user>/`. The original answers from Garmin are kept
too (compressed), so Dreeve can rebuild your health data when it learns to read more of them, without fetching
anything again.

## AI analysis

The Insights page builds a fixed summary of your week. Later, an AI model can use that same summary to explain how
your week went. That is off by default and only happens if you switch it on, see [AI assistant](/integrations/ai/).
