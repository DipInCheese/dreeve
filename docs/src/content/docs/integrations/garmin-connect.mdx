Dreeve fetches your activities straight from Garmin Connect. Every user connects their own Garmin
account from the browser, under **Manage → Garmin**. No settings files, no separate tools.

```
Garmin Connect → health-sync → watch/ → Dreeve imports it
```

:::note[Important]
This uses Garmin's unofficial connection, the one the Garmin Connect website itself uses. Garmin does
not support it and has changed it before, so expect an occasional break until Dreeve is updated.
:::

## Setup

The `health-sync` service is part of the multi-user
[docker-compose.yml](/getting-started/multi-user/). It runs from the same image as the app:

```yaml title="docker-compose.yml"
  health-sync:
    image: ${DREEVE_IMAGE:?Set DREEVE_IMAGE in .env}
    container_name: dreeve-health-sync
    restart: unless-stopped
    environment:
      TZ: ${TZ:-UTC}
    volumes:
      - ./data:/var/www/users
    command: ["health-sync"]
    networks:
      - dreeve
```

It publishes no ports: only the app talks to it, over the internal Docker network, with a secret the
app generates on its first start (`data/.health-sync-secret`).

## Connecting your account

1. Open **Manage → Garmin**, or **Connect Garmin** on an empty dashboard.
2. Enter the email and password of your Garmin account, and pick how far back to fetch: the last
   3 months, the last year, or everything.
3. If Garmin asks for a security code (sent by email or shown in the Garmin Connect app), enter it
   within 5 minutes.

Your password is used once, to log in, and is never stored. Dreeve keeps the session Garmin hands
out, in `data/<user>/garmin/`, readable by its owner only. It refreshes itself; when Garmin ends it,
the Garmin page asks you to log in again and nothing synced so far is lost.

## How syncing works

- Every hour health-sync asks Garmin for new activities and downloads the original `.fit` file of
  each. The files land in your `watch` folder and are imported right away.
- A long history comes in batches of 30, one batch every 10 minutes, so Garmin does not rate limit
  your account. The Garmin page shows when it is still catching up.
- **Sync now** on the Garmin page starts a sync right away.
- Activities without a `.fit` file (for example ones you typed in by hand on Garmin) are skipped.
- **Disconnect Garmin** deletes the session. Your synced activities are kept.

Five failed logins in 15 minutes pause further attempts, so Garmin does not lock your account.
