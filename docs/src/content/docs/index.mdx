## Key Features

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Garmin, connected in the browser">Activities, sleep, HRV, stress, Body Battery and weigh-ins, synced every hour</Card>
  <Card title="Today">Last night and today at a glance, every number next to your own normal</Card>
  <Card title="Health">Charts per topic over a week, a month, three months or a year</Card>
  <Card title="Insights">Your week summed up: averages against your normal, sleep rhythm and training</Card>
  <Card title="Private and multi-user">Every page needs a sign-in; one installation serves the whole household</Card>
  <Card title="Import activities">Use raw FIT/TCX/GPX files from your watch or app</Card>
  <Card title="Dashboard">See all your stats and charts at a glance</Card>
  <Card title="Automation rules">Automatically fill in gear, commute flags, sport types and more</Card>
  <Card title="Activities">Browse a detailed list of everything you've done</Card>
  <Card title="Monthly View">Monthly stats with an interactive calendar</Card>
  <Card title="Gear stats">Track how much you've used each bike, shoe, etc.</Card>
  <Card title="Maintenance Tracking">Keep tabs on gear wear and tear</Card>
  <Card title="Eddington">For your distance milestones</Card>
  <Card title="Segments & Efforts">Dive into your segment history and times</Card>
  <Card title="Heatmap">Visualize where you’ve been active the most</Card>
  <Card title="Milestones">A timeline view of your key achievements and milestones over time</Card>
  <Card title="Rewind">A fun way to look back on your year in motion</Card>
  <Card title="Activity Photos">Relive your moments with a photo archive</Card>
  <Card title="AI coach (optional)">Chat about your sleep, recovery and training; off by default, local models supported</Card>
  <Card title="PWA support">Use it like a native app on your phone</Card>
</CardGrid>
