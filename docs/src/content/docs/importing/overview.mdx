---
title: "Importing activities"
description: "How activities get into Dreeve: from .fit, .tcx and .gpx files."
---

Dreeve builds everything it shows you (dashboard, charts, heatmap, gear stats) out of your activities.
They come from `.fit`, `.tcx` and `.gpx` files: upload them in the browser, or drop them in the `watch` folder.

Importing from files has no external dependencies: your data never leaves your machine, nothing can
rate-limit you, and nothing breaks when an API changes.

```bash
> docker compose exec app bin/console app:import:files
```

:::note
The **daemon** container imports new files automatically, within seconds of their arrival.
:::
